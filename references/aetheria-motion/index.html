Each film is a web page that draws any frame for a given time (window.renderAt(t)); the renderer captures every frame in headless Chrome and muxes video and audio with ffmpeg. The 3D film's picture is rendered in Blender; the steps are in demos/04-3d-render/README.md. Font files are not in the repository; assets/fonts/README.md lists the ones needed. Without them the lettering will differ from the films.
